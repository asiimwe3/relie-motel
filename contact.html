Good to Know

Policies

Payment policy

Pay at the hotel in UGX cash or mobile money (MTN MoMo / Airtel Money). A booking via WhatsApp confirms your room — no card needed.

Cancellation

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before arrival on standard rates. Advance-purchase and group rates may differ — we'll confirm in writing when you book.

Check-in / check-out

Check-in from 14:30 (earlier when available, just ask). Check-out by 10:00. Late check-out on request, subject to availability.

Children & extra beds

Children of all ages are welcome. Extra-person and extra-bed fees may apply — confirm with the front desk when booking.

Parking & security

Free, private, on-site parking — no reservation needed. The premises are secured and the front desk is staffed 24/7.

Questions

FAQ

How far is Kibale National Park?

About 13 km — roughly a 30-minute drive. We help arrange permits, guides and early-morning departures for trekking.

Do you arrange airport pick-up?

Yes. Shuttle pick-up from Kasese airstrip (55 km) or Entebbe on request, at a surcharge. Tell us your flight details on WhatsApp.

Is breakfast included?

Yes — free continental/Western breakfast is served daily from 07:00 to 10:30.

Can I pay with mobile money?

Yes — MTN MoMo and Airtel Money are accepted, plus UGX cash.

Do you host conferences and groups?

Yes — we have a conference room and group rates for tour operators, schools, churches and weddings. See the Groups page or request a proposal.
