Welcome to Fort Portal

Discover Relie Motel

Welcome to Relie Motel — the leading and best-loved motel in Fort Portal, western Uganda. With 26 spotless rooms, improved amenities and the best services in Uganda's Tourism City, we open our doors to travellers from every corner of the world. Whether you fly in from Europe, trek over from Kenya or drive up from Kampala, you check in as a guest and leave as family.

From our warm 24/7 front desk to our chef's fresh local and international dishes and an in-house massage room to unwind in, every detail is crafted to make your stay memorable — with the crater lakes, Kibale National Park and the Tooro Kingdom Palace right on your doorstep.
