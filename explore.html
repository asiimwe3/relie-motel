Getting here

We're on Malibo Road, central Fort Portal (M72C+PHQ)

From Entebbe/Kampala

5–6 hours by road via Mubende, or fly to Kasese airstrip (55 km) and we pick you up.

From Kasese

About 1 hour north — through the foothills of the Rwenzoris.

Airport pickup

We arrange shuttle pick-up and drop-off on request (surcharge applies).
