New At Relie

The Massage Room

After a long flight, a chimp trek through Kibale or a day on the crater lakes, come down and let our trained therapist work the road out of your shoulders. Our in-house massage room offers relaxing full-body and back, neck & shoulder massages in a calm, private setting.

  • Professional massage therapy on site
  • Relaxing, deep-tissue and post-safari recovery massages
  • Open daily — book at reception or from your room
  • Open to resident guests and walk-in visitors
