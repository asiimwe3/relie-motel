# Relief Motel Website — 90-Day Sprint Plan (Oct–Dec 2026)

**Project:** Premium website + direct-booking channel for Relief Motel, Fort Portal City, Uganda
**Prepared by:** DeryCode Technologies · October 2026
**Mission:** A world-class website that brings international tourists to Relief Motel, fast — first foreign-guest inquiries within 7 days, direct bookings live within 90 days.

**Positioning:** *"Your base for exploring Uganda's Tourism City."* Clean, secure, affordable, central — 15 minutes' walk from the Tooro Kingdom Palace, 13 km from Kibale National Park chimpanzee trekking.

## Situation (from RESEARCH.md)

- 26 rooms, 2-star, USD 24–63/night. Google 3.8 (288 reviews), Trip.com 7.6, MakeMyTrip 5/5.
- Loved for: affordability, kind staff, customer care, security, quiet, spacious rooms, central location, restaurant & bar.
- Zero owned digital presence: no website, no domain, no email capture. 100% OTA-dependent.
- Fort Portal is Uganda's official Tourism City; global travelers search "Kibale National Park hotels", "Fort Portal crater lakes stay" and land on OTA pages, not on Relief Motel.

## THE FIRST WEEK (Days 1–7): visible to the world

Goal: foreign travelers can find and contact Relief Motel directly within 7 days.

1. **Day 1:** Register reliefmotel.com (fallback: reliefmotelfortportal.com); Google Business Profile full overhaul — categories (Motel + Hotel), attributes (free breakfast, free parking, Wi-Fi, restaurant), 20+ existing photos uploaded, exact location pin, both phone numbers (+256 778 472498, +256 704 023485), booking link = WhatsApp click-to-chat in English.
2. **Day 2:** One-page multilingual landing site live (English + French + German): hero photo, room rates, WhatsApp Book Now button, map, phone, "near Kibale" pitch. IndexNow + Google Search Console verification same day.
3. **Day 3:** WhatsApp Business profile set up with catalog (Double Room, Twin Room, rates) and quick replies in English/French/German.
4. **Day 4:** Google Ads campaign live targeting long-tail international keywords ("hotel near Kibale National Park", "Fort Portal motel", "cheap accommodation Fort Portal Uganda") in US/UK/EU/Canada/Australia — small budget (UGX 150k–300k/day) because these keywords are cheap.
5. **Day 5:** Tripadvisor + Google review link embedded everywhere; owner starts answering the most recent Google reviews.
6. **Day 6:** Pitch to Kibale/Semuliki tour operators and Fort Portal tour companies: "commission deal — send your guests to Relief Motel, direct WhatsApp number." Tour operators are the fastest foreign-guest channel in Uganda.
7. **Day 7:** Measure: impressions, clicks, WhatsApp messages received. Expected first foreign inquiries from ads + GBP this week.

## Days 8–30: Full premium website

1. Complete site: Home, Rooms (Double, Twin with photos, amenities, rates), Dining (restaurant + bar + menu), Amenities, Gallery, Location (getting here from Entebbe/Kasese), Explore Fort Portal (Kibale chimps, crater lakes, Amabere Caves, Semuliki, Tooro Palace), FAQ, Contact.
2. Premium design standard: mobile-first, sticky Book Now bar, cinematic hero, warm hospitality palette, AVIF/WebP images, LCP < 2s, Lighthouse 95+, WCAG AA.
3. Structured data: Hotel/LodgingBusiness + Restaurant + FAQPage schema; sitemap; hreflang for en/fr/de.
4. Booking = WhatsApp + contact form + phone (all confirmed live), with email notification to staff.
5. Multi-currency display (USD/EUR/GBP/UGX), English/French/German/Spanish/Italian/Chinese translations (machine + human review).
6. GA4 + conversion events (WhatsApp click, call, form submit), Meta pixel, heatmap.
7. GBP posting cadence: 2 posts/week; 20+ new photos from a proper shoot.

## Days 31–60: Direct booking engine + payments

1. Availability calendar; 3-step checkout (dates → room → guest details), guest checkout.
2. Payments: PesaPal (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, cards) + Stripe (international cards) for deposits; pay-at-hotel fallback.
3. Email/SMS/WhatsApp confirmations in the guest's language; clear cancellation policy.
4. Admin dashboard (Supabase): rooms, rates, availability, bookings, gallery, menu, reviews — staff logins, mobile-friendly for the 24/7 desk.
5. Review generation: automatic post-stay WhatsApp asking guests to review. Target 3.8 → 4.0 by day 90.

## Days 61–90: Global growth engine

1. Google Ads scaled on what converted; retargeting visitors; "best rate direct" guarantee vs OTAs.
2. Tour-operator partnerships formalized (commission codes tracked in dashboard).
3. Airport pickup add-on bookable; e-visa guidance page for foreign guests.
4. 6 destination blog posts live (Kibale trekking guide, crater lakes tour, Semuliki hot springs, Tooro culture, 3-day Fort Portal itinerary, bird watching).
5. Email capture + newsletter; past-guest re-booking offer.
6. Virtual tour (room + grounds) embedded on Rooms page.

## Risks

1. Photos are the weak point — schedule the shoot by Day 3; no stock shortcuts.
2. Week-1 foreign inquiries likely come via WhatsApp/ads/GBP, not SEO; SEO compounds from weeks 4–12.
3. Rate parity with OTAs — compete with value (free breakfast, airport pickup, best-rate guarantee), not underpricing.
4. Review score 3.8 caps conversion — operational fixes (bathroom toiletries quality) must run in parallel.
